- **P2 works as designed:** L2 had 120 min of `Paro programado` on Sep 30 (available shifts = 2.75). Production per available shift is normal (z = −0.20). Measured per shift worked (2,816 / 3 = 938.7 vs. a mean of ~1,012), it would have raised a false production alert (z ≈ −3.9).
- L2 scrap rate went from 7.03% to 7.46% after P2: the planned stop reduced the production of shift 3 (normal scrap), so shifts 1 and 2 (A1, ~9%) weigh more in the day's ratio of totals. The scrap rate of each shift did not change.
- L3 has no alert: A3 (Sep 10) is in the past and A4 (Sep 30) is normal.
- L3's scrap std (1.31%) is higher than the other lines because A3 is part of its baseline.
- The L2 scrap baseline (3.37%) is above the normal 3.0% because A1's Sep 28 and 29 are part of it.
